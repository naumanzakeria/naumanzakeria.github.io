Technology Doesn't Build Customer Loyalty. Relationships Do.

Organizations invest heavily in technology, but technology alone doesn't guarantee business outcomes. Success happens when customers confidently adopt solutions, build trusted partnerships, and keep expanding their investment because they see measurable value. That's where I create value — turning successful implementations into long-term customer partnerships that strengthen retention, increase adoption, and drive sustainable account growth.

What I Do

Areas of Expertise

Helping organizations improve customer adoption, experience, and retention through Customer Success, Customer Experience, and Customer Service leadership.

01 · Onboarding

Adoption & Onboarding

Guiding enterprise customers from implementation to confident, everyday use — turning a signed contract into a working partnership.

02 · Retention

Customer Health & Retention

Reading engagement, CSAT, and NPS signals to catch risk early, before it becomes churn — and keep relationships on track.

03 · Growth

Renewals & Expansion

Partnering with Sales to identify renewal, upsell, and cross-sell opportunities that grow accounts without eroding trust.

04 · Escalations

Executive Relationship Management

Acting as the senior escalation point for complex issues — coordinating cross-functional teams to resolve them fast.

05 · Insight

Voice of Customer

Turning customer feedback into product and process improvements that benefit the customer and the business alike.

06 · Leadership

Coaching & Team Development

Mentoring Customer Success Advisors and Support Specialists to build a culture of ownership and customer-first thinking.

07 · Experience

Customer Experience Design

Mapping the full customer journey and fixing the handoff points where trust quietly leaks — not just the moments with a project budget attached.

08 · Service

09 · Client Services

Client Services Management

Running the day-to-day operational relationship for enterprise clients — staffing, service levels, and consistent delivery behind the scenes.
