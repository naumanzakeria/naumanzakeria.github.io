Professional Summary

Technology is easy to buy. Trust isn't. With 15+ years across Customer Success, Customer Experience, Customer Service, and Client Services, I've built that trust from every seat — the front-line call resolving a customer's immediate problem, the journey redesign that keeps them coming back, and the enterprise relationship worth $1.5M+ in ARR that has to be earned again every renewal cycle.

Whether the job is answering a customer directly, improving the experience they move through, or managing the full client relationship end to end, my approach doesn't change: understand what the customer actually needs, remove what's standing in their way, and follow through until they see measurable value. That's how a Customer Service call becomes a Customer Experience win, and how a Customer Experience win becomes a Customer Success renewal — the same commitment, viewed from different seats.

I'm equally suited to Customer Success, Customer Experience, and Customer Service leadership roles — including Customer Experience Manager, Customer Service Manager, and Client Services Manager positions — having operated across all four functions throughout my career.
