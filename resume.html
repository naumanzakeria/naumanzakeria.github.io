Executive Value Proposition

Technology Doesn't Build Customer Loyalty. Relationships Do.

Technology can enable the customer journey. People, trust, and outcomes determine whether customers stay.

With 15+ years of experience across Customer Success, Customer Experience, Customer Service, SaaS, FinTech, Workforce Management, and customer operations, I have built my career around one principle: sustainable customer relationships are created when organizations combine exceptional experiences with measurable customer value.

My experience spans the complete customer lifecycle from onboarding and adoption to service delivery, customer health, escalations, retention, advocacy, and growth alongside leading customer-facing teams and improving the operational systems behind the customer experience.

I bring together three capabilities that organizations often manage separately: Customer Success, Customer Experience, and Customer Service. This allows me to understand not only what customers need, but also the people, processes, data, and cross-functional execution required to consistently deliver it.

I measure success by more than customer satisfaction. I look at whether customers adopt, achieve value, stay, grow, and ultimately advocate for the relationship.
