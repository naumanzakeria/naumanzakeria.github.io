About

It's Never Been About the Ticket. It's Always Been About the Relationship.

That's the lesson 15+ years across Customer Success, Customer Experience, and Customer Service has taught me — the same principle wearing three different job titles.

With more than 10 years of experience across Customer Success, SaaS, FinTech, and enterprise operations, I help organizations transform successful implementations into long-term customer partnerships that strengthen retention, increase adoption, and create opportunities for sustainable account growth.

I partner with customers and cross-functional teams to simplify onboarding, accelerate product adoption, resolve complex business challenges, and turn customer insights into strategic improvements that benefit both the customer and the business.

If you're hiring for a Customer Success Manager, a Customer Experience Manager, a Customer Service Manager, or a Client Services Manager — I'd encourage you to keep reading. I've spent 15+ years doing the first three, often at the same time, and I've never seen them as separate jobs. They're the same commitment to the customer, viewed from different seats.

When customers achieve their goals, the business earns loyalty, renewals, expansion, and long-term growth.
